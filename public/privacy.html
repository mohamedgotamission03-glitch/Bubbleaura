Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bubble Aura: Pop & Match (the “game”), published by Cubellio4Fun (“we”, “us”), accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information. It applies to the Android app with package name com.cubellio4fun.bubblorashooter, distributed through Google Play and the Samsung Galaxy Store.

Summary

  • There are no accounts, sign-in, purchases, chat, or user-generated content.
  • We do not run a server for the game and do not receive your game progress.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and uses device and advertising data, as described below.
  • We do not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, SMS, call-log or health permissions.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on our Google Play and Galaxy Store listings (developer name: Cubellio4Fun). We aim to reply within a reasonable time.

Information stored only on your device

The game saves the following in the app’s private local storage: unlocked levels, best scores and stars per level, sound setting, number of bombs, the date and streak of your daily gift, and a count of completed runs used to space out interstitial ads. This information is not sent to us. It stays until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game.

The game may use your device’s vibration feature for feedback. It does not collect vibration data.

Information processed for advertising (Google AdMob)

The game includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK. When the game checks consent or requests, loads or shows an ad, Google may collect and process, among other things:

  • device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, and app set / instance identifiers used by Google SDKs;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device, operating-system, app-version, language and network information;
  • ad interactions (for example, ads shown, viewed or clicked) and your consent choices;
  • diagnostics, crash and performance information about the ad SDK.

Google uses this to serve, personalize (where permitted), measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep its services secure. We receive aggregated ad performance and revenue reports from Google; we do not receive information that identifies you. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads in the game

A banner ad may appear at the bottom of the screen. An interstitial (full-screen) ad may appear between levels, after every few completed runs and never during a shot. After running out of moves you may choose to watch an optional rewarded video to receive bonus moves; the bonus is given only after the ad network confirms that you earned it. Ads need an internet connection and may be unavailable. The game is fully playable without watching any rewarded ad.

Advertising ID

The app may declare the Google Play services advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) so that the Google SDK can use the advertising ID for advertising and measurement. You can reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). If you delete the ID, the game still works.

Consent and your privacy choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in some US states), the game shows Google’s consent form before requesting ads. You can accept or refuse. Closing the app is not treated as consent, and ads are not requested without a permitted consent status. When Google requires it, Settings → Privacy choices in the game reopens the form so you can change your choice at any time.

Where the law requires a legal basis, our basis for advertising-related processing is your consent; Google may rely on other bases described in its own policies.

Who information is shared with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share game progress with anyone. Advertising and consent data is processed by Google (and the advertising partners Google works with) under Google’s policies. We do not give advertisers or other third parties access to the game’s local data.

Retention and deletion

We hold no server copy of your data and have no account to delete. To remove local game data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Data processed by Google is retained under Google’s retention rules; you can manage or delete ad-related data at adssettings.google.com and in your device’s advertising settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, we usually cannot look up or export it ourselves; for data processed by Google, use Google’s privacy tools above. You can also contact us using the details above.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, using safeguards described in its policies.

Security

Local game data stays in the app’s private storage. Communications made by the included Google SDKs use encrypted connections such as HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are younger than that age, please play only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. The game is not part of the Google Play Families program.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same public address. The policy in the app matches this page. See also the Terms of Use.
