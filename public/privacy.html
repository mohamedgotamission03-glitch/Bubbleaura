Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cubellio, published by cubellio4fun, accesses, collects, uses, handles, and shares information. It applies to the Android game Cubellio, package name com.cubellio4fun.blockpuzzle. The developer name in the Google Play listing is cubellio4fun.

Privacy contact

The privacy point of contact is the support email published on the Cubellio Google Play listing. No separate email address, postal address, or company registration number was supplied for this policy, so none is stated here. Please use that Play listing email for privacy inquiries.

Accounts

Cubellio does not let you create an account, sign in, or submit a profile. Because there is no app account, there is no account to freeze or delete. You can delete the game data stored by Cubellio by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. That deletion removes the local progress, best score, and settings saved by the game.

Information stored only on your device

The game stores the current puzzle, score, cleared lines, unlocked levels, completed levels, best score, sound preference, and vibration preference in local app storage on your device. cubellio4fun does not receive, upload, or operate a server for that saved game progress. It remains until you clear the app storage or uninstall Cubellio, or until the device cannot save it.

Information accessed or processed for advertising

Cubellio includes the Google AdMob advertising SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform consent SDK. When the app requests an ad or checks consent, Google may process device and advertising information, which can include an advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google may infer approximate location from an IP address. Cubellio does not request the device location permission and does not collect precise GPS location.

The current play screen does not display a banner. An interstitial ad may be requested between completed puzzles. After a game ends, you may choose to watch a rewarded video. A revive is granted only when Google’s SDK confirms that the reward was earned and the video has been dismissed. Advertising requires an internet connection and may be unavailable. If no ad is available, you can restart normally.

Those advertising requests are used to show, measure, and limit ads, to check whether ads may be requested, and to present a consent or privacy-options form where required. They are not used by cubellio4fun to build a player account.

Parties data is shared with

Saved game progress is not shared by cubellio4fun with anyone. Advertising and consent information is processed by Google through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s partner-site information. cubellio4fun does not independently sell personal or sensitive user data. “Sell” means exchanging that data with a third party for money. The developer requires advertising providers not to sell personal or sensitive user data collected from Cubellio.

Identifiers the app does not use

Cubellio does not collect or link persistent device identifiers such as IMEI, IMSI, or SIM serial number to personal data or to a resettable advertising identifier. The app itself does not use Android App Set ID for advertising, measurement, analytics, or fraud prevention. It does not access contacts, photos, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, health data, financial information, government ID numbers, or an inventory of other installed apps.

Security

Local game data stays in the app’s private device storage. Advertising and consent communications made by the included Google SDKs are sent using modern encrypted connections, such as HTTPS. No security method can be guaranteed against every risk.

Retention and deletion

Local puzzle data and settings are kept until you clear Cubellio’s storage or uninstall the app. cubellio4fun does not keep a separate server copy of that progress. Information processed by Google for advertising or consent is retained by Google under Google’s own retention rules. There is no Cubellio account whose data can be frozen instead of deleted.

Your choices

Where Google’s consent form is required, Cubellio presents it before requesting ads. Accepting or refusing that form requires an affirmative choice. Closing the app is not treated as consent. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings includes a control that opens it. You can also limit ad personalization in your device settings. Sound effects and vibration can be turned off in Settings. These choices may reduce ad availability but do not block normal puzzle play.

Children

Cubellio is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13, and it is not presented as a Google Play Families app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date will change and the replacement policy will be published at this same public page. The policy inside the app is intended to match this page.
